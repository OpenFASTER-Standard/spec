OpenFASTER
Standards Implementations About

OpenFASTER is a vendor-independent family of open standards for the harmonized data exchange of regulatory reporting, tax compliance and audit data, starting with MiKaDiv and FASTER. See About OpenFASTER for scope, terminology, and versioning policy.
